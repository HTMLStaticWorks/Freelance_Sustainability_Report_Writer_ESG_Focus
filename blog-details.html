The CSRD Countdown: What Non-EU Companies Need to Know

Sarah Jenkins

Lead ESG Consultant • 8 min read

The Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD) is not just an EU matter. For many non-EU companies with significant operations in Europe, the countdown to compliance has already begun.

Who is Affected?

Even if your company is headquartered in the US or Asia, you may fall under CSRD scope if you meet certain revenue thresholds within the EU or have large subsidiaries operating there.

"Transparency is no longer a choice; it is a global regulatory requirement that directly impacts capital access."

Key Compliance Steps

Navigating CSRD requires a systematic approach to data collection and framework alignment.

  1. Double Materiality Assessment: Identifying both the financial risks of climate change to your business and your business's impact on the environment.
  2. Gap Analysis: Comparing current disclosures against ESRS (European Sustainability Reporting Standards).
  3. Assurance Readiness: Ensuring your data is audit-proof for limited assurance requirements starting in 2025.
