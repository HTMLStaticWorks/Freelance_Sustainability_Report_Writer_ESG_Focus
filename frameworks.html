GRI Standards

Most Widely Used

The Global Reporting Initiative (GRI) provides the world's most widely used standards for sustainability reporting, focusing on an organization's impact on the economy, environment, and people.

Compliance Process:
  • Define reporting scope based on GRI Universal Standards.
  • Conduct double-materiality assessments.
  • Prepare GRI content index and mapping.

Benefit: Enhanced global comparability and transparency for multi-stakeholder audiences.

SASB / ISSB

Investor Focused

The Sustainability Accounting Standards Board (SASB) focuses on industry-specific standards that are financially material, helping businesses communicate ESG data to investors.

Compliance Process:
  • Identify industry-specific disclosure topics.
  • Measure accounting metrics against sector benchmarks.
  • Align with the new ISSB (IFRS S1 & S2) requirements.

Benefit: Directly addresses the needs of capital markets and institutional investors.

TCFD Disclosure Support
